    <!-- Intro Text Paragraph -->
    <p class="intro-text">
      This ongoing project involves converting a traditional gas-powered minimoto into a high-performance electric machine from the ground up. I removed the original small 4-stroke engine to clear space for a custom-engineered electric powertrain, battery pack, and modified frame geometry.
    </p>
